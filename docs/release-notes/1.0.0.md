## Pilothouse 1.0.0

The first public release of Pilothouse — drive the Claude Code CLI on your Mac
from your iPhone or iPad.

> Pilothouse works with Claude Code. It is an independent open-source project
> and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Anthropic.

### Mac app

* Menu bar app that starts and supervises `claude` sessions for your paired
  devices (stream-json, `--resume`, process groups, SIGINT/SIGTERM handling)
* QR pairing with a one-time, 5-minute token; paired devices can be revoked
  instantly
* End-to-end encryption between your devices (Noise IK over WebSocket),
  Bonjour discovery on port 47823
* Permission prompts forwarded to your phone through a local MCP server;
  unanswered requests are denied after 10 minutes
* Keeps the Mac awake while Pilothouse runs; optional lid-closed mode through
  a minimal privileged helper, switched off automatically on battery
* Tailscale assistant — let Claude set it up for you, or follow the guide
* Session logs in `~/Library/Application Support/Pilothouse`, kept for 30 days

### iPhone & iPad app

Available separately on the App Store (TestFlight first).

### Install

Download `Pilothouse-1.0.0.dmg` below (signed with Developer ID and notarized),
or use Homebrew:

```sh
brew tap ardasnturk/pilothouse https://github.com/ardasnturk/pilot-house
brew install --cask pilothouse
```

Requirements: macOS 15 or later and the official
[Claude Code CLI](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code/setup), signed in
with your own account.

`SHA-256` of the DMG: see `Pilothouse-1.0.0.dmg.sha256`.
