## Pilothouse 1.0.0

The first public release of Pilothouse — drive **Claude Code**, **Codex** and
**Gemini CLI** on your Mac from your iPhone or iPad. No server, no account, no
telemetry: your own devices, end-to-end encrypted.

> Pilothouse is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with,
> endorsed by or sponsored by Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.

### Highlights

* **Every agent you have** — start chats with Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI
  in any folder of your Mac; create new project folders from the phone.
* **Approve from anywhere** — edits with diffs, commands, *always allow*;
  unanswered requests are denied after 10 minutes.
* **Sub-agents** — Claude can hand tasks to Codex or Gemini; follow every
  sub-agent live in its own window and approve its actions.
* **`/` for skills and commands** — a searchable list with descriptions and
  where each comes from.
* **Weekly summary and limits** — what you worked on, written on the Mac by
  Apple Intelligence (macOS 26), plus Claude and Codex usage limits with reset
  times.
* **Notifications** on the Mac and, through ntfy, on your phone.
* **Anywhere access** with Tailscale; the Mac stays awake, optionally with the
  lid closed.

### Install

Download `Pilothouse-1.0.0.dmg` below (signed with Developer ID and notarized
by Apple), or use Homebrew:

```sh
brew tap ardasnturk/pilothouse https://github.com/ardasnturk/pilot-house
brew install --cask pilothouse
```

Requirements: macOS 15 or later and at least one agent CLI installed and
signed in — [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code/setup),
Codex or Gemini CLI.

**iPhone and iPad:** install through TestFlight:
[testflight.apple.com/join/6CE4VRRE](https://testflight.apple.com/join/6CE4VRRE)
(or scan the QR code on [pilot.ardasnturk.com](https://pilot.ardasnturk.com)).

The full list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/ardasnturk/pilot-house/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
