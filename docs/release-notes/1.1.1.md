## Pilothouse 1.1.1

### Fixed

* **Same Wi-Fi works.** On recent iOS versions the iPhone and iPad app could
  only reach the Mac through Tailscale: iOS cut its WebSocket connections to
  local addresses right after they were made. The app now runs the WebSocket
  protocol itself over a plain connection, so pairing and chatting work
  directly over your Wi-Fi again. Nothing to change on your side; the Mac app
  works with old and new phone versions.
* **Clearer pairing errors.** When the Mac cannot be reached, the pairing
  screen now also shows what happened for each address it tried.
* **The DMG window** shows its design again.

### Install

Update the iPhone or iPad app through TestFlight. On the Mac, use
**Settings → Updates → Install and Relaunch**, download
`Pilothouse-1.1.1.dmg` below, or run `brew upgrade --cask pilothouse`.

The full list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/ardasnturk/pilot-house/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
